What it's like here

You'll be on orchard blocks, council depots and clearing jobs as often as you're in the office. If you'd rather understand one range properly than read a catalogueue of twenty, you'll fit.

The kinds of people who do well

Diesel fitters, ag mechanics, contractors coming off the tools, and sales people who can read a hydraulic spec sheet without being walked through it.

What we don't need

A long résumé of unrelated industries. If you can't tell a drum from a disc yet, that's teachable - wanting to learn it isn't.

How to apply

A short note about what you've worked on and a résumé to sales@dte-equipment.com.au. No portals, no forms with fourteen fields.
