Who we are

A authorised partner that has actually been on the job.

DTE started in 2017 with a simple observation: most vegetation attachments sold in Australia were picked from a catalogue by people who had never run one. Wrong flow, wrong carrier, wrong finish - and a contractor left to work it out in the paddock.

So we went the other way. We hold two brands, not twenty. We know what each machine does in mango canopy, in leucaena rows, on a council verge and in standing timber, because we've been there when it was cutting.

'Work Smarter' isn't a slogan on the wall. It's the test we put every attachment through before we'll put our name on it.

The support that continues after purchase

This is the part a catalogue can't sell you, and the reason customers come back for their second and third machine.

01

Specced to your machine

We start with your carrier, your flow and pressure, and the job it has to do. Wrong hydraulics is the single most common reason an attachment disappoints.

02

Delivered and set up

Fitted, tuned and handed over running - not dropped on a pallet. Operators get walked through settings, tooth choice and what to watch in the first hundred hours.

03

Parts held locally

Tough jobs wear parts down. That's why we stock teeth, blades, and wear parts here in Australia, so you can get back on the job with minimal downtime.

04

We check back in

A call a few weeks in to see how it's cutting. It's the thing customers mention most in their reviews, and it costs us nothing but the phone call.
