Structures we see most

Which one suits you depends on your tax position and how long you'll hold the machine. Your accountant will have a view - we're happy to talk to them directly.

Chattel mortgage

You own the attachment from day one and it sits on your balance sheet. The most common structure for contractors and growers buying to keep.

Finance lease

The financier owns it and you lease it for a fixed term with a residual. Useful when you'd rather keep the asset off the books.

Rental / rent-to-own

For a single contract or a season you want to prove out before committing. Talk to us early - availability is limited.

Seasonal payments

Repayments weighted to when the money comes in - after harvest, after the contract is paid. Ask for it; it isn't always offered.
