PTO driven - the standard build

S and PLUS models carry their own pump and oil tank, driven off the PTO at 540–750 rpm. Your tractor supplies no oil - PTO, weight and mounting are what matter.

Hydraulic driven - Basic Front

Scorpion 330–630 and Spider 520 / 620 can be built without a pump, running on the carrier's hydraulics instead. Cheaper where the machine already has the flow, and best suited to hedge and tree work.
